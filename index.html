01 / Profile

Language is effortless—until you look closely.

My work examines multilingual lexical control: how speakers activate, select, and inhibit words across languages. I combine behavioral experiments with neuroimaging to connect patterns in response time and speech errors to the timing and location of brain activity.

Raised bilingually in English and French and later learning Spanish, I bring both scientific and personal curiosity to questions about multilingual experience.
